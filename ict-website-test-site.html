About the Project

A heist, investigated

The story: four masked robbers break into Mr Rashid's vault and steal an ancient crown. Detective Hamza takes the case, working with his intern Ayan and Sara, alongside Inspector Khan, to track the robbers down.

The production: the film was generated with AI from start to finish — video, voice over and music — then edited into its final 10-minute 54-second cut. A 10-scene story board planned every beat before generation began.

The course: made for ICT Open Ended Lab 1 at Dawood University of Engineering and Technology (DUET), Karachi, by a six-student team from the Computer Science Batch of 2026, supervised by Engr. Imran Alvi.
